# Biography
I am a Software Development Engineer at AMD, working on vLLM. Previously, I studied Computer Engineering at Purdue University, interned at Anyscale across 2022 and 2023, and worked on open-source multi-agent reinforcement learning with Farama Foundation.

I'm passionate about language models and reinforcement learning, and am a contributor to several open-source reinforcement learning libraries, including OpenAI Gym, PettingZoo, and RLlib.

In my free time, I can be found climbing, playing squash, or at the piano.
